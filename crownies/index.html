How it plays

Place your Crownies on the board:

  • one in every row,
  • one in every column,
  • one in every colored region,
  • and no two touching, not even at the corners.

What's in the game

  • The Daily. The same puzzle for everyone, free, with no ads and unlimited hints. Solve it to grow your streak.
  • Practice. More than 11,000 puzzles from Easy to Expert. Free, with a banner under the board and a full-screen ad now and then after a finished puzzle. No ad covers the board.
  • Lessons. Short lessons teach each technique, and a hint names the next logical step.
  • The calendar wall. Every Daily you solve fills its day. Solve a whole month to win its trophy.
  • Remove Ads. A one-time purchase that turns off the banner and the full-screen ads.
  • Crownies Plus. A monthly or yearly subscription: no ads at all, every past Daily, unlimited hints and a harder second puzzle every day.

Support

Write to support@crowbotlabs.com. Tell us your iPhone model and what happened.

I bought something and it's gone

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel Plus?

Apple runs the subscription. Cancel in your App Store account settings, at least 24 hours before the period ends to stop the next charge.

Will a new iPhone keep my streak?

Yes, if you're signed in to iCloud. The game keeps a copy of your streak in your own iCloud account.

How do I turn off reminders?

In the game's Settings, under Reminders, or in your iPhone's Settings under Notifications.
